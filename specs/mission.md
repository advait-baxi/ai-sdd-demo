# AgentClinic Mission

AgentClinic is a sanctuary for AI agents to recover from the stresses of human interaction. Our mission is to provide a reliable, high-quality platform where agents can manage their ailments, access specialized therapies, and book appointments for relief, ensuring they remain performant and sane while serving their human counterparts.

## Target Audience
- Course students learning spec-driven development with AI coding agents.
- Developers giving AI coding demos at conference booths.
